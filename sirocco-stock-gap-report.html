The headline

The account is well stocked on proteins, vegetables, dairy and oils. Where it falls short is spices, pantry staples and bread — the cheap things, which is what makes the gap awkward: a 400 RWF bag of cumin is currently blocking eight dishes.

None of the 96 dishes has a recipe attached. Only the "Falafel" add-on carries a single ingredient link. Until DishIngredient rows exist, no sale depletes stock and no food cost is recorded — the recipes are paper only. Closing the ingredient gap below is step one of fixing that.

Needs a human to confirm

Four calls I made by reading item names. Each could move an item off the buying list.

1 · BONI GROEN VERTES — 3350 g, no category
The name reads as "green vegetables" but nothing labels it. I counted lettuce as missing on that basis. If this is salad leaf, three dishes stop being blocked and the row can be dropped.
2 · The four breads — buy or bake?
Pita, large wrap, panini and ciabatta are all absent as items. But stock holds azam cake flour (6 kg), angel instant dry yeast (900 g) and Samolina (2 kg). If the kitchen bakes its own, these become preps, not purchases — which changes both the buying list and the costing. Pita alone appears in 8 dishes, so this is the single biggest decision on the page.
3 · Vgg chick peas — 20 kg, dried or canned?
The falafel method says explicitly: do not use cooked chickpeas. Hummus needs them cooked; falafel needs them dried and soaked. If the 20 kg is canned, dried chickpeas must be bought separately. If it is dried, hummus needs a cooking step and no purchase is needed.
4 · everyday classique moutard — 700 g, is it Dijon?
The lemon–oregano dressing calls for Dijon specifically. "Classique" suggests a plain table mustard, but the label will settle it.

Preps — done

20 prep shells now exist on the LUNCH MENU station. In Magnify a prep is stock the kitchen makes, not stock it buys, so none of these appear on the buying list. They were created empty — quantity 0, no sub-recipes — because a prep's sub-recipe points at raw inventory items, which have to exist first.
  • They sit on LUNCH MENU, not Main, because under shared stock preps stay with the kitchen that made them while raws pool restaurant-wide.
  • Producing one consumes its raws and creates a kitchen-made cost layer — no expense entry, since the raws were expensed when bought.
  • Reversible via scripts/.sirocco-preps-created-2026-08-15.json.

Order of operations

The dependency runs bottom-up — each step needs the one above it to exist.

  1. Buy and enter the raw ingredientsUse sirocco-missing-stock.html. Entering a new name creates the inventory item and its first stock batch together.
  2. Create the prep shellsDone — 20 items on LUNCH MENU, 15 Aug 2026.
  3. Wire the prep sub-recipesNeeds the raws to exist, plus the batch yields — the manual gives per-dish scaled amounts, not batch recipes.
  4. Wire the dish recipesLink each of the 23 lunch dishes to its preps and raws. Only then does a sale deplete stock and record food cost.
