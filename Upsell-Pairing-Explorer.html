How to read the explorer
  • Filters narrow, they do not restrict the answer. Picking a category chooses the dish you are asking about; the pairings that come back can sit in any menu, category or sub-category.
  • Lift is the honest ranking. Pair rate alone always crowns whatever is most popular — soda and water. Lift divides that out.
  • Profit, not revenue. Every figure is net of FIFO food cost, so a cheap high-margin add-on can outrank a pricier drink.
  • Thin numbers stay thin. 3 bills is enough to list a pairing, not enough to bet on it. A lift of 3.4 on 3 bills is a lead to test, not a fact.
  • Party size still confounds. A 6-guest table buys four things because it is six people. Read the big-table dishes with that in mind.
