Anything marked Not built must be either finished or removed from the accompanying Software Documentation before submission — an overstatement here is a certification failure, not a rounding error.

A · Connection to the VSDC

The clauses that decide whether a receipt may exist at all. Everything else is downstream of these.

B · Numbering and the journal

What RRA audits hardest. A repeated or skipped number is the defect these clauses exist to catch.

IDClauseRequirementTestExpectedStatus
B-01§7.3 Consecutive numbering, one ascending sequence per receipt type, no gaps. Issue 50 mixed receipts. Export the journal and check every sequence. Each type runs 1..n with no gap and no repeat. Built
B-02§7.3 Two tills billing simultaneously cannot take the same number. Settle concurrently from two terminals against one outlet, repeatedly. Numbers are consecutive across both. No duplicate at any load. Built
B-03§7.24.4, §7.25 Both counters print as A/B RT — per type, and across all types. Issue a sale after several refunds and copies. Read the printed line. e.g. RECEIPT NUMBER: 168/258 NS, both advancing correctly. Built
B-04§7.3 A number claimed by an abandoned sale must not leave a gap. Force a failure after the number is claimed but before the sale commits. The number is released with the transaction. The next sale takes it. Built
B-05§7.14 An electronic journal records every receipt; the system will not operate without it. Inspect the journal after a full trading day. Every receipt present, in order, with its amounts and signature. Partial
B-06§6.5 Complete journal data for NS and NR is sent to the VSDC in text form. Issue sales and refunds, then inspect what reached the VSDC. Journal text transmitted for every NS and NR receipt. Not built

C-07§13 Discounts reduce taxable amount and tax proportionally, and are declared per line. Apply 25% to one line of a mixed bill. dcRt 25, dcAmt and charge reconcile to the supply amount exactly. Built

E · Receipt types

Five types, and strict rules about which may be issued when. The refund rules are the operationally surprising ones.

IDClauseRequirementTestExpectedStatus
E-01§13 Normal sale (NS) is the ordinary receipt offered to the client. Settle a bill. Receipt labelled NS, signed, handed over. Not built
E-02§7.17 A transaction cannot be corrected without cancelling the original, which must be referenced. Refund a settled bill. Refund receipt prints REF. NORMAL RECEIPT# naming the original. Partial
E-03§7.17 An original may be cancelled only once. Refund a bill, then attempt to refund it again. The second attempt is refused, and says why. Partial
E-04§13.2 Refund amounts print negative throughout — lines, totals, tax and tender. Refund a mixed-bracket bill. Every figure negative, including per-bracket tax. Not built
E-05§7.18, §11 Only one original prints. A reprint is a COPY, watermarked, with the disclaimer. Reprint an issued receipt. COPY above the items and as a watermark; THIS IS NOT AN OFFICIAL RECEIPT below totals, at least twice the size of the amount text. Not built
E-06§11 Training and proforma tickets carry the same designation and disclaimer. Issue one of each. TRAINING / PROFORMA marked as above. Neither is signed. Not built
E-07§6.3.6 Training and proforma receipts are not digitally signed. Inspect a training ticket. No signature block. Not counted as a sale. Not built
E-08§12 All corrections are made before the receipt is approved. Amend a bill before settling, then settle. Amendments possible before approval, impossible after. Built
