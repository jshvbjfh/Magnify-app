1 · Scope

This agreement covers Magnify as installed at a user's premises: the till application, the manager application and the waiter tablet application, together with the configuration required for them to operate as a Certified Invoicing System.

It covers the software only. Section 6 states what it does not cover.

Support hours

08:00 to 23:00, seven days a week, including public holidays.

These hours are set to match when restaurants trade. A point-of-sale fault matters most during service, and service is largely evenings and weekends — support that stopped at six on a Friday would be of little use to a user.

A fault reported outside these hours is treated as reported at 08:00 the following day.

3 · Severity

Severity is set by the effect on the user's ability to trade, not by how the fault appears in the software.

LevelMeaningExamples
Critical The user cannot trade. Bills cannot be settled or receipts cannot be issued. Till will not start; receipts will not print; the system cannot reach the sales data controller
High Trade continues, but an important function is unavailable. Kitchen tickets not printing; one terminal down while others work; stock not deducting
Medium A function is impaired but there is a workaround. A report showing wrong figures; a screen behaving oddly
Low Cosmetic, or a request rather than a fault. Wording, layout, a question, a change request

What resolution means

A fault is resolved when the user can trade normally. That may be by correcting the fault or by providing a workable alternative while a correction is prepared. Where a workaround is used, the underlying fault remains open until corrected.

Targets, not guarantees. YOFINDER Ltd will use reasonable endeavours to meet the times above. They are not warranties, and failure to meet one does not itself create a financial liability — see section 8.

5 · How support is delivered

Remotely first. Most faults are diagnosed and corrected remotely, which is faster than travelling.

On site where remote work cannot resolve it. Where a fault requires attendance — and many do, since a till is a physical machine with cables, printers and power — YOFINDER Ltd will attend the user's premises within Kigali. Attendance outside Kigali is by prior arrangement.

Escalation

A critical fault not resolved within its target is escalated to the Managing Director, who will contact the user directly with a plan and an expected time.

6 · What this agreement does not cover

Stated plainly, because a user relying on cover that does not exist is worse served than one who knows its limits.

  • The user's internet connection, and any fault arising from its loss
  • Computers, tablets, printers, cabling, power supply and any other hardware
  • Electrical supply, including outages and damage caused by them
  • Third-party software or services on the same equipment
  • The Virtual Sales Data Controller and RRA's own systems
  • Loss or corruption caused by unauthorised changes to the software, its database or its configuration
  • Training beyond initial handover, unless separately agreed
  • Data entry, including menu setup and stock counting

A certified system will stop trading when the controller is unreachable. That is required by the specification, not a fault in Magnify. YOFINDER Ltd will assist in diagnosing the cause, but a controller or connection outside its control is outside this agreement.

7 · The user's part

Support depends on a few things the user provides:

  • Report faults promptly, by phone where urgent, describing what happened
  • Allow remote access when asked, and access to the premises where attendance is needed
  • Maintain a working internet connection and functioning hardware
  • Keep supervisor credentials secure and disclose them to no one outside the business
  • Do not alter the software, its database or its fiscal configuration except through the application
  • Accept updates required for continued compliance

Where a fault arises from any of these not being met, the response times in section 4 do not apply, though YOFINDER Ltd will still assist.

8 · Remedies and limits

Where YOFINDER Ltd repeatedly fails to meet the response times in section 4, the user may escalate to the Managing Director and, if the failures continue, terminate on 30 days' written notice without penalty.

The response and resolution times are targets pursued with reasonable endeavours. They do not create liability for loss of profit, loss of trade or other indirect loss. Any liability that does arise is limited to the support fees paid in the preceding twelve months.

Nothing in this agreement limits liability where the law does not permit it to be limited.

9 · Updates and versions

Corrections and improvements are issued as new versions of Magnify. A certified installation does not update automatically: each version is installed deliberately, so that an installation cannot move past the version RRA has approved.

Where RRA requires a change for continued compliance, YOFINDER Ltd will provide the updated version and the user will install it. Where a change is required urgently, it is treated as a critical item.

The software documentation accompanying Magnify is revised for every change, as the specification requires.

10 · Term

This agreement runs alongside the user's licence for Magnify and continues for as long as that licence is in force. Either party may terminate on 30 days' written notice.

On termination, YOFINDER Ltd will provide the user with an export of their data in a readable format. Fiscal records are retained by the user as the law requires.
