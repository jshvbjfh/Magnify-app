2 · The system

Magnify is a point-of-sale and inventory system for restaurants, supplied by YoFinder Ltd and operating as a Certified Invoicing System connected to a Sales Data Controller.

The version submitted for certification is 1.1.50. The version in use is shown within the application, transmitted with every request to the supplier's server, and printed on every receipt, so that it can be verified by Authority personnel as clause 7.7 requires.

A fault within Magnify preventing a taxpayer from issuing receipts is treated as critical in every case.

Times are targets pursued with reasonable endeavours, on the terms of the Software Support SLA, and are measured within support hours in Central Africa Time. They are not warranties.

A taxpayer may also be unable to issue receipts for reasons outside Magnify — an outage of the Sales Data Controller, of the Authority's systems, or of the taxpayer's own connection or equipment. YoFinder Ltd will assist in identifying the cause in every such case, but cannot undertake to resolve within four hours a fault in a system it does not operate.

4 · Compliance undertakings

YoFinder Ltd undertakes that, for as long as Magnify is certified:

  • The software will perform no function beyond those stated in its documentation, as clause 7.10 requires, and the documentation will be revised for every change.
  • Each release will carry a unique version identifier, verifiable by Authority personnel.
  • Certified installations will not update automatically. Each version is installed deliberately, so that no installation moves past the version the Authority has approved.
  • No function will be provided by which VAT can be disabled, a settled sale altered or deleted, a receipt number reused or reset outside a full system reset, or a receipt issued without a response from the Sales Data Controller.
  • Fiscal records will be retained and will not be removable by any function available to a user, save for the transfer-of-ownership reset which clause 7.2 itself requires. That reset is refused unless the records have first been exported and delivered to the taxpayer, so no fiscal record is destroyed that has not been preserved in the taxpayer's own hands.

Should a defect be discovered which permits any of the above notwithstanding this undertaking, YoFinder Ltd will report it under section 6, treat it as a fault of critical priority, and correct it in a version notified under section 5.

5 · Notification of change

YoFinder Ltd will notify the Authority before releasing any change that affects:

  • the calculation of tax or of any amount printed on a receipt
  • receipt numbering or the sequence of any counter
  • the content or layout of any receipt
  • the daily reports or the journal
  • communication with the Sales Data Controller

Notification will be given not less than 14 days before release, with a description of the change and the revised documentation. The change may be released at the end of that period unless the Authority objects within it. Where the Authority requires a longer period or prior approval for a particular class of change, YoFinder Ltd will observe it once notified.

Changes not affecting the above — corrections to unrelated functions, or changes to appearance — are recorded in the documentation and reported to the Authority on request.

Where the Authority requires a change for continued compliance, YoFinder Ltd will prepare it, notify affected users and assist with installation. Such a change is treated as being of critical priority for response and communication, and the updated version is delivered within the timeframe the Authority sets.

6 · Assistance to the Authority

YoFinder Ltd will, on request and within a reasonable time:

  • demonstrate the system to Authority personnel
  • provide the audit interface required by clause 7.26, showing software settings, records and the state of each counter
  • assist an audit of any certified installation
  • supply the current documentation and the current version identifier
  • report any defect discovered that affects the accuracy or completeness of declared sales

The last of these applies whether or not the Authority has asked. A defect is reported once confirmed, not on first suspicion, and within these times:

Where the defectReported within
Affects figures already transmitted to the Authority48 hours of confirmation
Affects accuracy or completeness otherwise5 working days of confirmation

A report states what was found, which installations and periods are affected so far as known, and what is being done. Investigation continuing does not delay the report.

7 · Continuity

Ceasing to supply means ceasing to support existing certified installations. Declining to take on new users is not a cessation and does not engage this section.

Should YoFinder Ltd cease to support existing certified installations, it will notify the Authority and every affected user not less than 90 days beforehand — or, where events beyond its control make that impossible, including insolvency proceedings, as soon as is practicable.

It will provide the export described below and reasonable assistance in transferring each installation to another certified system, for a period of up to 90 days from that notice.

Fiscal records held by any installation will be exported and delivered to the taxpayer in a readable form before support ends.

8 · Term

This undertaking takes effect on the date of certification.

It continues for as long as any certified installation of Magnify remains in use, meaning an installation operating under a current Software Support Agreement with YoFinder Ltd. An installation whose agreement has ended is no longer a certified installation for this purpose.

It ends on the earlier of the Authority withdrawing certification, or the later of: 90 days after notice given under section 7, and performance of the obligations in that section.
