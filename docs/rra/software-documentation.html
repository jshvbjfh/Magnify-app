1 · Purpose and scope

Magnify is a point-of-sale and inventory management system for restaurants. It records orders, settles bills, prints receipts, tracks stock and produces management and fiscal reports.

This document covers Magnify 1.1.50, the fiscal build, which operates as a Certified Invoicing System connected to a Virtual Sales Data Controller. Non-fiscal builds of Magnify exist for use outside Rwanda; they are a different product and are not covered here.

2 · System architecture

Magnify is three applications sharing one database. Only one of them issues receipts.

ApplicationPlatformRole in the CIS
Till
Magnify POS		 Windows · Electron This is the Certified Invoicing System. It settles bills, calls the VSDC and prints receipts. It is the only component that issues a receipt.
ManagerWeb · Electron Configuration, menu, stock, staff and reports. Does not issue receipts.
TabletAndroid Order entry only. Has no printer and issues no receipt. Orders are sent to a till, which settles them.

The certified surface is the till alone. The tablet cannot print and therefore cannot issue a receipt; the manager application does not settle bills. Confining receipt issuance to one component keeps the certified boundary unambiguous.

Data flow for a sale

Waiter enters order  ->  Kitchen ticket printed
                     ->  Bill presented (proforma)
                     ->  Till settles the bill
                         -> journal entry written (PENDING)
                         -> sale sent to local VSDC
                         -> VSDC returns SDC id, receipt numbers,
                            internal data and signature
                         -> journal entry confirmed (SENT)
                         -> fiscal receipt printed
                     ->  VSDC relays to the RRA EBM server

The receipt is printed only after the VSDC has answered (clause 10). Should the VSDC not answer, no receipt is printed and the sale is not completed.

Changing the taxpayer

The TIN cannot be edited. It may be changed only in service mode, and only as part of a reset which erases every fiscal receipt, all receipt numbering and the cash drawer history held under the previous TIN (clause 7.2). Receipt numbering then recommences from 1 (clause 7.3). The operation requires a named approver and an explicit confirmation of the erasure.

5 · Fiscal operation

5.1 Tax

Menu prices include VAT. Tax is extracted from the price rather than added to it, so the amount a guest is charged is the amount shown on the menu. Each item carries a tax type of A, B, C or D; unclassified items are treated as standard rated.

Each line is rounded to two decimal places and the order total is the sum of the rounded lines. Per-bracket tax is computed from the bracket total rather than by summing rounded lines, so the printed figures reconcile with one another.

5.2 Receipt numbering

Two counters are maintained per outlet: one per receipt type and one across all types, printed as A/B RT (clause 7.24.4). Numbers are claimed inside the settlement transaction, so a sale that fails leaves no gap. Counters never reset except on a total reset of the system, when they recommence from 1.

5.3 Receipt types

5.4 Immutability

A settled bill cannot be cancelled, deleted or edited. The only means of reversing a sale is a refund receipt referencing the original, and an original may be reversed once only.

5.5 Journal

Every receipt is recorded before it is printed. Should the journal be unwritable, or should any printed receipt be unrecorded, the till stops trading (clause 7.14).

5.6 Stock

A receipt is not issued for goods when the recorded stock is less than the quantity requested. Service items are exempt (clause 7.30). Items whose stock has never been counted are not treated as out of stock.

6 · Connection to the Sales Data Controller

This section is stated separately because it is the one part not yet verified. The message format and everything that surrounds it are built and tested. The transport and its failure behaviour require a controller instance, which has been requested from the Authority.

The till communicates with a Virtual Sales Data Controller over REST. Sales are sent to /trnsSales/saveSales. The response supplies the SDC identifier, the per-type and total receipt counters, the internal data and the receipt signature, which are printed in the SDC information block.

Built and verified

  • The sales message itself, in the format defined by the VSDC API specification. Its figures reproduce the worked example in that specification, and the per-bracket rounding matches the printed receipt in clause 13.1.
  • The journal entry written before printing, held pending until the controller answers and confirmed or failed on its response.
  • Receipt numbering, claimed within the settlement so that a failed sale leaves no gap.
  • The receipt layout, including the SDC information block, the internal data and signature grouped in fours, and the QR payload composed as clause 7.24.7 defines.

Awaiting a controller instance

The following are specified and cannot be completed or demonstrated without a Sales Data Controller to communicate with:

  • Sending the message and receiving the response (clause 7.19, 7.20).
  • Withholding the receipt until the controller has responded (clause 10, 7.8).
  • Detecting the controller's availability independently and reporting its status to the user (clause 7.9).
  • Ceasing operation on a communication error and resuming when it clears (clause 24.7.9).
  • Transmitting journal data for sale and refund receipts (clause 6.5).

Behaviour cannot be tested against a controller that does not exist, and YoFinder Ltd will not describe as verified what it has not verified. A test instance has been requested; this section will be completed and this document revised once the integration has been demonstrated.

7 · Access control

Staff sign in by PIN on shared terminals or by username and password for personal accounts. Both are stored as one-way hashes and cannot be recovered; a forgotten PIN is reset, not retrieved.

Supervisor approval, entered as a separate PIN and recorded by name, is required to cancel an order, remove a sent dish, apply a discount, move a line between bills, take cash out of the till, issue a refund, and change the TIN.

8 · Data, retention and audit

Fiscal receipts, cash movements and counter state are retained indefinitely and are never deleted by any function available to a user. Ordinary orders may be hidden from management views by date without being removed from the database.

The audit interface presents software identity and version, fiscal configuration, tax rates as programmed, record counts by receipt type, the state of each counter, and any receipt awaiting or having failed transmission.

9 · Support and updates

The software version is shown in the application, transmitted with every request to the server, and printed on the receipt. Certified installations do not update automatically; a new version is installed deliberately, so that an installation cannot move past the version RRA approved.

This documentation is revised for every change to the software, as clause 7.7 requires of the version and clause 7.10 requires of the documentation.

10 · What the system does not do

  • It does not register a sale without printing a receipt.
  • It does not print a receipt without a response from the controller.
  • It does not permit a sale amount to be entered without identifying an item.
  • It does not allow VAT to be disabled by any setting or user action.
  • It does not allow a settled bill to be cancelled, edited or deleted.
  • It does not allow an original transaction to be reversed more than once.
  • It does not reset or reuse a receipt number.
  • It does not issue receipts from the tablet or the manager application.
