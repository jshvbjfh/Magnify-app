Magnify has been built directly against the *Technical Specification of CIS for
VSDC* and the *VSDC API Specification*. Of the fifty-two requirements set out
in the Test Cases document, **thirty-four are built and covered by automated
test**, two are properties of the host equipment, and **sixteen await something
the Authority issues**. The Test Cases document states which is which, and why,
requirement by requirement.

The tax arithmetic reproduces the worked example in clause 13.1 exactly,
including the per-bracket rounding that yields 814.58 rather than 814.57 on the
sample bill.

**We would be grateful for the following, which the remaining sixteen depend
on:**

1. **Access to a test Sales Data Controller.** The message format and the
   receipt layout are complete; the exchange itself cannot be built or
   demonstrated without a controller to communicate with. The sandbox portal at
   myrratest.rra.gov.rw does not accept our TIN, 149047989, so we are unsure of
   the correct route to request this.

2. **Confirmation of tax categories C and D** — the rate and the supplies each
   covers. A and B are clear from the specification; we have no worked example
   of D.

3. **The RRA logo artwork**, in a form suitable for thermal printing at 58mm
   and 80mm, as clause 7.29 requires on every receipt.

4. **Guidance on the watermark required by clause 11** for copy, training and
   proforma receipts. Thermal printers do not overlay text natively, and we
   would rather follow the Authority's expected practice than choose our own.

We would be glad to demonstrate the system to your technical staff at any
stage, at your offices or ours.
