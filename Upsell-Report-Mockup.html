About this report

Attachment, not conversion. Attachment rate measures how often two items appear on the same bill. It does not prove a waiter offered the second item. Nothing in the database records an offer that was declined, so a 0% rate may mean a waiter never asks — or that their tables never want one.

What counts as an upsell. Add-ons, sides and desserts, plus drinks. Drinks are measured only against bills that had food on them: a guest who came in for two beers was not upsold a drink, that was the visit.

Benchmarks come from your own floor. Profit opportunity compares each pairing against the waiter who already achieves the best rate on it here, over at least 5 bills. No industry targets are assumed.

Comparisons need volume. Waiters under 20 bills and pairings under 15 are shown but not ranked.

Scope. Whole restaurant account, every station. One bill routinely spans stations and it is the bill that gets upsold. Guest QR orders are excluded — nobody was at the table to suggest anything.
