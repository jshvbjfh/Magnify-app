What these numbers do and do not prove
  • Attachment, not conversion. Nothing records a guest saying no.
  • 126 bills. Rates built on 13–40 bills move with a handful of orders.
  • Revenue, not profit. Menu price before discount and food cost; the live report subtracts FIFO cost.
  • Waiter rows are illustrative — names and bill counts are real, the per-category splits are shaped, not measured.
  • Party size confounds every attach rate. A 6-guest table buys four things because it is six people, not because anyone upsold. Brochette (5.1 guests) and Plancha (6.1) top the table for that reason. Rates should be read per cover, not per bill.
  • 262 paid orders exist in total. Association mining normally wants thousands; the structure works now, the numbers get trustworthy later.
  • Before any of this, Sirocco needs fixing: 43 of its 62 drinks are read as food, so its attach rates are wrong today.
